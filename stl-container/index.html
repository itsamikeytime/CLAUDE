The hollow grows as large as it can while keeping every wall at least this thick.

Inside surface

Size of a ball rolled around the inside. Bigger gives a rounder, more bowl-like inside but thicker walls in places; walls never go below the minimum.

4 Lid

Gap between the plug and the opening on every side. About 0.15–0.25 mm suits most FDM printers; 0.1 mm for resin.

The insert rises into a pocket in the lid top, like the plug goes into the container. The clearance applies here too: the insert is that much narrower than the pocket and that much shorter, and so are the pins.

Holes are made wider than the pins by the clearance, and 0.5 mm deeper, to leave room for glue. Pins go only where the holes keep a solid wall around them; smaller pins are used if these don't fit.
